3

Assign Initial Investigation Owner

Route using symptoms & evidence to target the correct specialist unit

Symptom / Evidence Observed Likely Root Cause Assigned Owner / Action
Journal in Primo, but page states "No Subscription" Coverage date mismatch or unactivated portfolio Investigate Coverage & Subscription Entitlement
Database link redirects to wrong destination / 404 Malformed URL parser syntax or broken Alma portfolio link Acquisition & Alma Team
Off-campus users fail to authenticate via OpenAthens Attribute release failure, domain restriction, or proxy loop Authentication & Systems Team
Publisher confirms subscription expired / payment issue Lapsed license agreement, renewal delay, or unpaid invoice Licensing & Acquisition Team
Multiple unrelated platforms/publishers failing simultaneously Shared infrastructure failure (LibKey/OpenAthens, SSO, DNS) Systems / Network Infrastructure (Treat as Incident)
4

Investigate & Collaborate Where Necessary

Cross-team coordination rules & accountability

Single Lead Ownership Rule

The initial assigned owner retains ticket leadership throughout multi-department inquiries. Do not bounce tickets endlessly between teams without clear actions or named owners.

Avoid "Ticket Ping-Pong": Never reassign a case back without specific test results or explicit required actions.

Example Cross-Team Escalation

Scenario Publisher claims "No Active Subscription," but library records show paid invoice.
Action Licensing Team verifies entitlement with vendor while Collection/Tech Team double-checks Alma e-resource link parameters & IP ranges.
