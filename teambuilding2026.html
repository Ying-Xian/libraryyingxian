Workshop Facilitation Architecture

Primo NDE Discovery Workshop Blueprint

A 5-question sequence engineered to build psychological safety, surface friction anonymously, and end with concrete cross-team action.

Est. Duration: 25–35 Minutes
STEP 1 Word Cloud

👍 What Works Well?

Warm-up exercise to generate positive energy and identify key wins to protect.

Goal: Positive baseline
STEP 2 Open / Anon

🤔 What Doesn't Work?

Anonymous open text to capture confusing, frustrating, or labor-intensive issues.

Goal: Uncover friction safely
STEP 3 Open Text

💡 What Could We Improve?

Brainstorm enhancements across search, access, AI, guidance, and collections.

Goal: Wide feature ideation
STEP 4 Pivot Question

🤝 Cross-Team Handoffs

Crucial pivot question linking Discovery with Reading Lists, Doc Delivery, Metadata & AI.

Goal: Inter-team alignment
STEP 5 Ranking / Poll

⭐ Prioritise Opportunities

Participants choose their top 2 initiatives to produce a concrete action list.

Goal: Clear priority outcome

Question 3 • Type: Open Text with Category Prompts

💡 What could we improve?

Facilitator Prompt: "What would make discovery, access or support easier for our users?"
Display these topic badges on your presentation slide to inspire ideas:
🔍 Search 🔑 Access 📚 Reading Lists 🚚 Resource Sharing 📂 Collections 🤖 AI Discovery 📖 Guidance 💬 Communication
Question 4 • ⭐ The Essential Pivot Question

🤝 Where could we work better across teams?

Facilitator Prompt: "What could Primo NDE help us connect, coordinate or improve together?"

This pivots the session from "what is wrong with Primo?" to "what can we build together?"

Synergy Pair 1

Reading Lists + Discovery

Direct reserve links in Primo result lists & real-time reading pack availability.

Synergy Pair 2

Resource Sharing + Discovery

1-click RapidILL fallback options when items are not locally owned.

Synergy Pair 3

Academic Services + Discovery

Subject guide integration & embedded librarian help widgets in Primo UI.

Synergy Pair 4

Metadata + Collections + Discovery

Enhanced indexing rules for specialized local archives & digital collections.

Synergy Pair 5

Research Support + AI Discovery

Responsible AI citation summaries & research repository indexing in Primo.

Question 5 • Type: Multiple Choice / Ranking Poll

⭐ Which opportunity would make the biggest difference to our users?

Facilitator Announcement: "Review our ideas above and vote! Choose your top 2 options. This provides us with our concrete priority agenda."
Max Choices: 2 per person

Event Configuration Cheat Sheet

Q1. 👍 What do you think is working well with Primo NDE? Word Cloud / Open Text

Prompt: "What helps our users? What helps your team? What should we keep?"

Q2. 🤔 What doesn't work as well as it could? Open Text (Enable Anonymous)

Prompt: "Think about things that are confusing, frustrating, difficult to find, or create extra work for users or staff."

Q3. 💡 What could we improve? Open Text

Prompt: "What would make discovery, access or support easier for our users? (Search, access, Reading Lists, resource sharing, collections, AI, guidance, communication)"

Q4. 🤝 Where could we work better across teams? Open Text

Prompt: "What could Primo NDE help us connect, coordinate or improve together? (e.g. Reading Lists + Discovery, Resource Sharing + Discovery, Metadata + Collections)"

Q5. ⭐ Which opportunity would make the biggest difference to our users? Multiple Choice (Select Max 2)

Instruction: "Show submitted ideas from Q3/Q4 as options. Tell everyone: Choose your top 2."
